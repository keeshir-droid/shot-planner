SHOT PLANNER: HOW TO USE IT
===========================

What it does: you paste your reel script into Claude, and it tells you
exactly what to film, where to put your phone, and why.

You need: a Claude account with Skills turned on, and a phone and tripod.
Nothing else to install.


STEP 1: DOWNLOAD
----------------
1. On this GitHub page, click the file named "shot-planner.zip".
2. Click the download button (an arrow pointing down) at the top right.
3. The file lands in your Downloads folder.

Do NOT unzip it. Claude wants the zip file as it is.


STEP 2: ADD IT TO CLAUDE
------------------------
1. Open Claude (claude.ai) and sign in.
2. Click your name or picture, then "Settings".
3. Open "Capabilities" (it may be called "Features").
4. Find "Skills" and make sure it is switched on.
   If you see "Code execution", switch that on too.
5. Click "Upload skill" (or "Add skill") and choose shot-planner.zip.
6. Switch the skill on. You should see "shot-planner" in the list.

The menu names can be a little different on your screen. Look for
Settings, then Skills.

Updating? Delete the old shot-planner skill first, then upload the new zip.


STEP 3: USE IT
--------------
1. Start a NEW chat in Claude.
2. Paste your script and say what you have around you. For example:

   "Plan the shots for this reel. I want it punchy. I've got a laptop,
   a desk and a coffee. No clip-on mic."

3. Claude gives you a shot plan: a table of shots, where to put your
   phone, and links to example videos.
4. Ask for changes in plain words:
   "Make it calmer."   "Why is shot 3 a close-up?"   "I have a mirror too."


TIPS
----
- The more you tell Claude about what is around you, the better your shots.
- Turn on web search in Claude to get links to real example videos.
- If Claude does not use the skill, say: "Use the shot-planner skill".
